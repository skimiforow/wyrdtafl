Wyrdtafl — Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

This policy covers the Android game Wyrdtafl — The Judgement of the Norns, published by Skimiforow, an independent developer. The same text is readable inside the game under Settings → About → Credits & privacy.

Who publishes the game

Wyrdtafl is published by Skimiforow. Questions about this policy: sersight+wyrdtafl@gmail.com.

What the game collects

Nothing about you. The game needs no account, and no personal data is collected, stored by us or sold. Your progress and settings are kept on your device (and, only if you sign in to Google Play Games, in your Google account: see below). Uninstalling the game removes them, unless your device's own Android backup (if you have it switched on) restores them when you reinstall.

Crash reports

When the game fails it can send a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a service that helps us fix bugs. A report holds:

  • the error and its stack trace;
  • the game version;
  • the device model and the Android version.

It holds no name, email, location or advertising identifier, and Sentry is set not to store your IP address. Crash reports are optional: switch them off in Settings → Reports.

"Report a problem"

The report form sends only the text you type, linked to the last error of that session, so we can read your words next to the technical details. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. Test versions of the game may also ask you, once, to rate it from 1 to 5; those ratings and any comment you add are sent to Sentry the same way, and only if you tap Send.

Permissions

The game uses three permissions that Android grants without asking: Internet and network state, used solely to send crash reports and problem reports, and vibration, for the haptic feedback (which you can switch off in Settings). It plays fully offline.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends Google your unlocked Deeds (achievements), your best scores (leaderboards) and a copy of your game progress (a saved game), which Google keeps in your Google account under its own privacy policy. We do not receive any of it. Signing in is optional; you can sign out in the Play Games app and manage or delete your Play Games data in your Google account.

No ads, no tracking

Wyrdtafl shows no advertising and uses no analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs beyond Sentry and the optional Google Play Games.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Retention and your rights

Sentry keeps crash events for 90 days by default, then deletes them. You may ask us by email to delete any report, and you can stop new ones at any time in Settings.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published in the game and on this page.
