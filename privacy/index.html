Wyrdtafl — Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

This policy covers the Android game Wyrdtafl — The Judgement of the Norns, published by Skimiforow, an independent developer. The same text is readable inside the game under Settings → About → Credits & privacy.

Who publishes the game

Wyrdtafl is published by Skimiforow. Questions about this policy: sersight+wyrdtafl@gmail.com.

What the game collects

Nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, and no personal data is collected, stored by us or sold. Your progress and settings are kept only on your device; uninstalling the game removes them.

Crash reports

When the game fails it can send a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a service that helps us fix bugs. A report holds:

  • the error and its stack trace;
  • the game version;
  • the device model and the Android version.

It holds no name, email, location or advertising identifier. Crash reports are optional: switch them off in Settings → Reports.

"Report a problem"

The report form sends only the text you type, linked to the last error of that session, so we can read your words next to the technical details. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

Permissions

The game asks for a single permission, Internet, used solely to send crash reports and problem reports. It plays fully offline.

No ads, no tracking

Wyrdtafl shows no advertising and uses no analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs beyond Sentry.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Retention and your rights

Sentry keeps crash events for 90 days by default, then deletes them. You may ask us by email to delete any report, and you can stop new ones at any time in Settings.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published in the game and on this page.
