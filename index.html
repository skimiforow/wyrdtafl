Wyrdtafl — The Judgement of the Norns

A Norse tile duel for Android. Two tiles in your hand — one of Fire, one of Ice. Keep them in balance, bet your Wyrd, cast the Galdr Stones, and sit the table of every realm before the Norns pass judgement.

Single player · 1 to 3 AI champions · Portrait and landscape · Plays offline · No ads, no in-app purchases

What happens at the table

Every hand is two tiles, Embers and Frost, each worth I to VI. The difference between them is your Dissonance. After three omens the Norns judge every hand: the lowest Dissonance wins, a pair is Harmony, and the losers pay in Wyrd Stones.

ᚠ

Meditate or Forge Destiny

Keep your tiles for free, or bet a Wyrd Stone to draw from a deck or a face-up Pyre. Every draw is a gamble with the balance of your soul.

ᛟ

Wyrd Stones

Your fate, in pebbles. Winners take their stake back from the Altar; losers feed the Well of Urd. Reach zero and you are banished to Helheim.

ᛉ

Galdr Stones

Three spells carried into every match, each usable once: Harvest stones back, strike your rivals with Wrath, or bend the omens themselves.

ᛚ

Loki and Yggdrasil

Wild tiles with no value of their own. Loki rolls two bone dice and lets you keep one; Yggdrasil lets you name the value — if you dare.

ᚱ

The Crossing

Nine realms, nine tables, one thread of Wyrd. Face each realm's champion on their own ground, from Midgard to Asgard, on four difficulties up to Ragnarök. Completing it unlocks new stones and clan relics.

ᚹ

Valhalla and the Daily Table

Hold the table of the fallen wave after wave in Valhalla, and sit the same seeded Daily Table as every other traveller to beat your own best.

ᛗ

Mímir's Tree, Deeds and Saga

Spend Honor Echoes on permanent power, chase Deeds, and read everything the Norns have written about you so far.

ᛞ

Skins and totems

Tiles in Roman numerals, pips, tally notches or runic calendar staves; bone, amber or meteoric-iron dice; stones, tables and animated totems that react to your play.

Made of code and candlelight

Everything on the table is drawn and sounded by code: the hewn rune stone, the bronze Wheel of the Norns, the physics of the bone dice, the embers of Muspelheim and the rime of Niflheim. A synthesised Nordic score and the voices of the Norns follow every turn.

Haptics on impacts and choices. Designed for phones in either orientation and for tablets and desktop mode. No account, no connection needed to play.
