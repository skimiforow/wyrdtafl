The one rule

Lowest Dissonance wins.

Every hand is two tiles: one of Embers, one of Frost, each worth I to VI. The gap between them is your Dissonance. A pair is Harmony — and Harmony is hard to beat.

  1. Meditate or Forge Destiny

    Keep your tiles for free, or bet a Wyrd Stone to draw from a deck or a face-up Pyre.

  2. Cast your Galdr

    Three rune spells per match, each used once: harvest stones back, strike with Wrath, bend the omens.

  3. Face the Judgement

    After three omens the Norns rank every hand. Losers feed the Well of Urd. At zero Wyrd, Helheim waits.

Galdr Stones

Twenty spells. Carry three.

Choose the stones you take into every match, each usable once and no more than two of a kind. Every cast shakes the table with runic light.

  • Harvest — win Wyrd Stones back from the Altar.
  • Wrath — strike every rival's Reserve at once.
  • Chaos and the Veil — bend the omens, or hide your hand.
  • Awaken them in Mímir's Tree for a stronger form.

Loki & Yggdrasil

Wild tiles. Real dice.

Some tiles have no value of their own. A Loki tile rolls two bone dice — with real physics, tumbling across the stone — and lets you keep one. A Yggdrasil tile takes the value you need, if you dare to trust it.

  • Bone, amber or meteoric-iron dice.
  • Haptics on every impact.

Made of code and candlelight

Forged by one pair of hands.

The hewn rune-stone table, the bronze Wheel of the Norns, the embers of Muspelheim and the rime of Niflheim: everything on the table is drawn by code, in real-time 3D, with physics for the dice and particles for every spell.

Questions

Before you sit down.

Is it free to play?

No — Wyrdtafl is a paid game with a single price. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no loot boxes and no energy timers. Everything is earned by playing.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The game plays fully offline and needs no account. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and only adds achievements, leaderboards and cloud save.

Is there online multiplayer?

Not at the moment. Wyrdtafl is single player: you face one to three AI champions, each with their own style of play. The Daily Table gives every player the same seeded table to compare.

Which devices does it support?

Android phones, foldables and tablets, in portrait or landscape, plus Samsung DeX and desktop mode with mouse and keyboard. Graphics settings from Light to Max keep it smooth on modest hardware.

Which languages?

English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Polish and Turkish.

Do I need to know Norse myth or tafl games?

Not at all. A guided first match teaches the rules in a few rounds; the myth is there to enjoy, not to study.
