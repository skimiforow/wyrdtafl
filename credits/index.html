Wyrdtafl — Credits

Wyrdtafl — The Judgement of the Norns

Publisher

Skimiforow — an independent developer from Portugal. One table, one pair of hands.

Design, code, art direction and audio

Skimiforow. Everything on the table is drawn and sounded by code: procedural textures, shaders, particles and sound effects. The hall theme “Wyrdtafl” was composed by Skimiforow; the table music is a synthesised Nordic score. The voices of the Norns and the champions were synthesised with Azure Neural TTS.

Built with

React, three.js (react-three-fiber and drei), Framer Motion, Tailwind CSS, Vite, Capacitor and Sentry.

The Cinzel typeface by Natanael Gama is used under the SIL Open Font License; the licence text ships with the game as fonts/OFL-Cinzel.txt and with this site as assets/OFL-Cinzel.txt.

Thanks to the open-source community: this table stands on your work.
