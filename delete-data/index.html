Wyrdtafl — Delete your data

This page explains how to have your data deleted from Wyrdtafl — The Judgement of the Norns, the Android game published on Google Play by Skimiforow.

The game has no accounts, so there is no account to close. The only data that ever leaves your device and reaches us is described below, and you can ask for all of it to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  • 1. Send an email to sersight+wyrdtafl@gmail.com with the subject "Wyrdtafl data deletion".
  • 2. Because the game knows nothing about who you are, help us find your reports: tell us your phone model and, roughly, the dates you played. If you sent a "Report a problem", quote a few words of what you wrote.
  • 3. We delete the matching data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Crash reports sent automatically when the game fails: the error, its stack trace, the game version, the device model and Android version, and a random installation identifier used to group reports from the same installation.
  • Problem reports: the text you typed in Settings → "Report a problem".

Nothing is kept after deletion. Even without a request, crash and problem reports are deleted automatically 90 days after they are received. To stop new reports, switch off Settings → Reports in the game.

Data on your device

Your progress, settings and unlocks are stored only on your phone. Remove them with Android Settings → Apps → Wyrdtafl → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the game.

Google Play Games

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your achievements, scores and cloud save are kept by Google in your Google account, not by us. Delete them in the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data) or from your Google account.
